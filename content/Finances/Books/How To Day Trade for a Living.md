### Mindset

Day trading is difficult and to be successful requires patience, dedication, practice, and emotional control. Additionally it's recommended to start blogging about your trades as it can help with self-reflection and increasing your own understanding as it's the most important element to endeavor.

> How many doctors and surgeon do you know who memorize the names of all their patients, their prescribed medications, their medical history, and a summary of each appointments they've ever had? None. They all have excellent record-keeping tools, some mandated by their profession and some personally chosen. A successful and consistent profitable trader is the one who keeps excellent records about their trades. 

In day trading it's important to work on simple, well-known strategies but adjust them overtime to your own personality.

### Rule 1

**Rule 1 - Day Trading is not a strategy to get rich quickly.** Stay away from anyone who thinks the stock market are designed to help you get rich overnight. Many people has the misconception about day trading being easy but if it was easy the failure rate wouldn't be at 90%.

> It takes time to become a consistently profitable trader. Many traders will fail in the long and at times fatal day trading learning curve.

The 90% failure rate scares and disappoints a-lot of people but the failure rate for startups, new businesses, and chemical engineering students are at 90%. If you want to make a-lot of money it's not going to be easy.

----

### Rule 2

**Rule 2 - Day Trading is not easy. It's a serious business and you should treat it as such.** Day trading is not a hobby, you will need to study and prepare to enter the world of day trading as seriously as a student would while in the university.

In day trading you're competing against professional traders from Wall Streets and other experienced traders around the world who are serious, highly equipped with advanced educations and tools and most importantly committed to making money.

* **Business Plan:** Always prepare your business plan and strategies that you will use. Additionally, invest the money you have for equipments such as education, computer, monitors, and scanner software, platforms, and other tools.
* **Education:** Always trains yourself to learn new things related to trading, humans, and anything that can give you edge over others.
* **Startup Capital:** You must spend money to make money.

As mentioned you will need to threat day trading as a real business if you're interested in succeeding in it.

### Rule 3

**Rule 3 - Day traders do not hold positions overnight. If necessary, you must sell with a loss to make sure you do not hold onto any stock overnight.** It's part of human nature to believe a stock will go up overnight but we may end up paying a heavy price for it therefore we should always close our positions at the end of the day.

The definition of **"Buying Long"** is that the trader has bought 100 shares of Apple Ince and would like to sell them for a higher price for profit. The definition of **"Sell Short"** means the trader has sold 100 shares of Apple Inc and would like to purchase them back for a cheaper price. **In day trading short selling is generally more dangerous but you can make a profit from it.**

### Retail Traders vs Institutional Traders 
 
 A individual trader is called a retail trader these are people who are not part of a firm or responsible for managing someone else's money. On the other hand institutional traders are people in Wall Street and their trades are mainly based on computer algorithms and high frequency trading. A individual trader has tremendous advantage over institutional traders because the individual trader can decide whether or not they want to trade.

> In guerrilla trading, as the term suggests, you are in hiding, waiting for an opportunity to move in and out of the financial jungle in a short period of time to generate quick profits while keeping your risk to a minimum. You don’t want to defeat or outsmart investment banks. You are simply waiting for an opportunity to reach your daily profit target.

Many hedge funds base their trading on sophisticated computer algorithms and high frequency trading (HTF). Many people has the misconception about high frequency trading where they believe it will beat them at all times but in reality none can predict the stock market 100% and most programmers implementing these programs are not day traders themselves. **You will need to find the moments where the computer algorithm cannot take your money - Find the entry. Make your move. Make your exit. Take your profit.**

> A now-classic example of the failure of this sort of HFT is presented in the book, One Good Trade, by my friend and acknowledged great trader, Mike Bellafiore. In September 2008, the investment bank Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. (ticker: LEH, now delisted after bankruptcy), Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corp (then ticker: FRE), and many other mortgage holdings and investment banks all suffered a massive drop in price. Programs tried to buy their already broken stock to squeeze and burn the short sellers, but the stock price never went higher. Day traders and huge institutional sellers dumped their shares on the program. The programs and their developers were obliterated and left holding huge quantities of worthless shares of LEH and FRE, as well as other bankrupted holdings.

**You will not get anywhere in day trading by complaining about computer algorithms. What will that accomplish? How will that help you make money?** It's better to put your energy in spotting patterns and understanding the market to beat the computer algorithm. **What a trader must never do is make excuses.**

### Trade the Best, Leave the Rest

**Trade the Best, Leave the Rest.** When the stock market moves up majority of the stocks moves up but when the stock market is going down majority of stocks are going down. However, there will always be handful of stocks that will move by themselves and these stocks are called for **Stocks In Play** and usually these are stocks which have unexpected fresh news.

* Earning reports
* Earning warnings and pre-announcements
* Earning surprises
* FDA approvals or disapprovals
* Mergers or acquisitions
* Alliances, partnerships, or major product releases
* Major contract wins or loses
* Restructuring, layoffs, or management changes
* Stock splits, buybacks, or debt offerings

All these can lead to the stock reacting in a different way than the stock market itself. An example if FDA approved a brand new drug the stock will experience growth while the stock market is crashing that day.

----

### Rule 4

**Rule 4 - Always ask "Is this stock moving because the overall market is moving or is it moving because it has a unique fundamental catalyst".** As a day trader focus on trading stocks which are moving every single day and receiving tons of actions. Additionally, use social media such as twitter, community of traders, and chatrooms to see which stocks are trending. Never trade by yourself use all the tools which are available for your advantage.

What day traders are looking for is stocks that has a big day such as releasing of earning reports, newsbreak, or had something bad or good happening to it. From the market opens at 9:30 AM to 11:30 AM the New York time will have the most trading volume and volatility.

**Ruel 5 - Success in day trading comes from risk management - finding low risk entries with a high potential reward. The minimum win:lose ratio for me is 2:1**.
