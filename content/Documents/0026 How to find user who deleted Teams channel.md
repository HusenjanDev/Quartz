## Introduction

A Teams Channel inside Microsoft teams were deleted around few days ago which contained important documents for our clients. When a Teams channel is deleted we shouldn't just recover it but investigate the reason the user deleted it.

## Solution

1. Open Microsoft Teams

2. Go to Teams Group.

3. Select Teams Group.

4. Select all Teams group.

5. Filter by deleted temas channel

6. Click on restore.

## Logs

We can also find out the user who deleted the Teams channel through the Microsoft Purview through the following steps.

1. Go to Microsoft 365 Portal

2. Go to Microsoft Purview

3. Click on eDiscovery

4. On field enter Teams channel name.

5. Click on Search and wait few minutes.

6. View the log and you will see who deleted Teams channel.

## Investigation

If the user tells us they didn't delete the Teams channel for safety reasons we should consider investigating their sign-in logs and their activity on their account such as password changes and etc... We will never know if the action was malicious or an accident to we do our investigation.
